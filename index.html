Practice Activity 1

Letter Naming Fluency (LNF)

Score student letter naming responses in real time

Examiner Script
"Here are some letters (point to the student form). Tell me the names of as many letters as you can. When I say 'Begin,' start here (point to the first letter), and go across the page. Point to each letter and tell me the name of that letter. If you come to a letter you don't know, I'll tell it to you. Put your finger on the first letter. Ready? Begin."
Reminders
Start timer: After you say "Begin"
Student hesitates: Wait 3 seconds; name the letter; point to next letter and say "Keep going"; mark as incorrect
Student says letter sounds: Say "Remember, tell me the letter's name, not its sound." Score as incorrect.
Discontinue: If student does not produce any correct letter names in the first line (10 letters)
